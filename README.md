# Healthcare Medical Equipment Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

This Power BI project analyzes medical equipment inventory across multiple hospitals and cities. The dashboard provides insights into equipment distribution, maintenance status, procurement costs, and operational efficiency, enabling healthcare administrators to make data-driven decisions regarding asset management and resource allocation.

## Business Problem

Hospitals manage a large inventory of medical equipment from different manufacturers. Tracking equipment status, maintenance requirements, procurement costs, and asset utilization can be challenging. This dashboard helps healthcare organizations monitor equipment performance and optimize maintenance planning.

## Dataset Information

The dataset contains 1,000 medical equipment records with information such as:

* Equipment ID
* Equipment Name
* Manufacturer
* Hospital Name
* City
* Quantity
* Unit Price
* Total Cost
* Purchase Date
* Equipment Status

## Dashboard Features

* Equipment inventory overview
* Total procurement cost analysis
* Equipment distribution by hospital
* City-wise equipment allocation
* Manufacturer performance analysis
* Operational vs Maintenance vs Out-of-Service status tracking
* Equipment quantity and cost breakdown
* Interactive filters and slicers

## Key Insights

* Identified equipment distribution across multiple hospitals and cities.
* Monitored operational, maintenance, and out-of-service equipment.
* Analyzed procurement costs to support budgeting decisions.
* Evaluated manufacturer contributions to hospital inventory.
* Tracked equipment purchases over time.
